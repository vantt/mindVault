2. Description of the Service

PassChef is a free, open-source Chrome Extension that generates passwords on demand from "recipe" formulas stored in your own Google Sheets. The extension combines a recipe (a formula you define) with an encrypted secret (a phrase known only to you) to derive a unique password client-side — entirely within your browser.

Key characteristics of the service:

  • All password generation occurs client-side. No passwords are transmitted to any server.
  • Encrypted secrets are stored in chrome.storage.sync (Google's infrastructure). The developer has no access to this data.
  • No account or registration is required.
  • The extension is provided free of charge under the MIT open-source license.

4. Master Password Responsibility

Your master password is the single key to your encrypted secrets. You bear sole and complete responsibility for:

  • Choosing a strong, memorable master password
  • Keeping your master password confidential
  • Securely backing up your master password and secret phrases offline
  • Not sharing your master password with others

There is no master password recovery mechanism. By design — and in keeping with zero-knowledge architecture — the developer cannot recover, reset, or bypass your master password under any circumstances. If you lose your master password, you will permanently lose access to your encrypted secrets and will need to reconfigure the extension from scratch. This is a fundamental feature, not a limitation to be worked around.

The developer strongly recommends writing down your master password and all secret phrases and storing them in a physically secure location (e.g., a locked safe or safety deposit box).

10. Security Considerations and User Responsibility

PassChef is a security tool, and its effectiveness depends heavily on your own security practices. You are responsible for:

  • Using a strong, unique master password that you do not use elsewhere
  • Keeping your operating system, browser, and the extension itself up to date
  • Not using PassChef on devices that may be compromised by malware or keyloggers
  • Understanding that the generated password exists in your clipboard for up to 30 seconds and may be accessible to other applications during that window
  • Understanding that if your master password is compromised, an attacker who also has access to your chrome.storage.sync data could potentially decrypt your secrets
  • Storing your master password and secret phrases in a secure offline backup

No password manager — however well-designed — is a substitute for good security hygiene.
