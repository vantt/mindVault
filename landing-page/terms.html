2. What the extension does

PassChef cooks Dishes from Recipes such as r4nd0m#1 in a Google Menu and private Spices in an encrypted Cookbook. Own-profile cooking combines ingredients and Spices according to the Recipe. Argon2id derives the Cookbook key from the master password; AES-256-GCM protects stored profiles. Legacy derived sharing uses additional derivation.

  • Encrypted profiles and readable metadata/preferences use Chrome Sync; unlocked key/lifetime/action data use session storage, and nonsecret attempt/onboarding records use local storage.
  • Google hosts the Menu and optional Sync. There is no separate PassChef password-backend account in the inspected extension.
  • Cooking alone does not copy. Dish Copy, Recipe-only Copy, encrypted exports and Full Access delegation are separate actions.
  • Current bounded source acceptance does not establish final Store installation, universal cross-device Sync, phone receiver support or a broad browser/OS/performance matrix.

4. Master password, Spices and recovery

Keep a strong master password and unpredictable Spices confidential. Maintain a secure recovery record and protect your device. The developer cannot recover a forgotten master password or reconstruct lost Spices. An encrypted Full Access copy plus its sharing password may preserve access to the Spices it contains; it is sensitive material requiring its own protection.

Reinitializing the extension does not restore old account passwords. Changing the Cookbook master password changes its protection, not the Dishes used on websites. To rotate an account password, change the relevant ingredient/Spice deliberately and update the account itself.

A final allocation of liability, any cap, remedies and consumer-rights disclosures requires publisher/legal approval. This draft does not assert a zero-dollar cap based on a free service or waive rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

6. Authorized use and sharing

Use Recipes, Menus and account credentials only where you have authority. Do not use the extension for unlawful access or disclose someone else's credentials without permission.

Full Access export/QR contains original profile Spices encrypted under a separate sharing password. A recipient with both can recover those Spices and cook the same Dishes. Share only with an authorized recipient. Deleting the source profile or changing its master password does not revoke independent exports, screenshots, recipient imports or account access; rotate affected account passwords and Spices as needed.

8. Google and other third parties

Google Sheets hosts your Menu; the browser extension reads selected Recipe content through page DOM access on its fixed host scope. Chrome Sync may transmit encrypted profiles and readable metadata under browser settings. Google services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy and applicable Google terms.

Google interface/API changes, browser policy, network conditions and storage quotas may affect operation. A public Menu can disclose account labels and patterns; a visible Tag can help test weak Spice guesses. Keep sensitive Menus private. The separate website also uses hosting and font providers as described in the Privacy draft.

9. Changes, removal and data controls

Update, support and discontinuation arrangements must be approved for the actual release. Do not assume removal from the Store, uninstalling, or discontinuation leaves your Cookbook recoverable or erases every copy.

Current Reset clears Chrome Sync and session storage after confirmation, not local attempt/onboarding records, Google Menu content, exported/recipient copies or OS clipboard history. It has no explicit offscreen clipboard-clear command. Chrome documents local storage removal on uninstall and session clearing on disable, reload, update or browser restart; cross-device deletion propagation and upgrade/uninstall/reinstall behavior still need selected acceptance. See the Privacy draft for current retention limits.

10. Security boundaries

Use strong credentials, trusted updated devices and deliberate recipient selection. Malware, phishing, stolen encrypted records, weak Spices and clipboard capture remain risks. The optional short Tag checks Recipe/Spice/Menu context without changing the Dish; legacy untagged Recipes remain usable and removing the Tag removes that check.

After Dish Copy, cleanup is attempted at 30 seconds only if the current clipboard still matches. Read denial, sleeping/closed Chrome, OS history and concurrent writes limit cleanup. There is no guarantee that a Dish cannot remain accessible beyond 30 seconds or that all memory copies are erased.
