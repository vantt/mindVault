Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026 PassChef / passchef.io

The short version

No passwords, secrets, or personal data are ever sent to any server. Everything stays on your device.
Encrypted secrets are stored in chrome.storage.sync — Google's own infrastructure. We never touch them.
No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking of any kind.
The clipboard auto-clears 30 seconds after a password is copied.
You own your data entirely. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the PassChef Chrome Extension ("PassChef", "the extension", "we", "us") handles information when you install and use the extension. PassChef is an independent, open-source project maintained by an individual developer.

PassChef is built on a zero-knowledge architecture. This means that by design, we are technically incapable of accessing your passwords, secrets, or any sensitive data — because that data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server we operate.

2. Data We Collect (and Don't Collect)

Data stored locally on your device

The extension stores the following data exclusively in chrome.storage.sync, which is managed by Google's Chrome infrastructure:

  • Encrypted secrets — your secret phrases, encrypted with Argon2id key derivation and AES-256-GCM encryption before being stored. The plaintext values are never written to disk or transmitted anywhere.
  • Encrypted master password verification data — a cryptographic salt and verification token derived from your master password. The master password itself is never stored.
  • Extension settings — non-sensitive preferences such as auto-lock timeout.

Data we never collect

  • Your master password (plaintext or otherwise)
  • Your generated passwords
  • The recipe formulas in your Google Sheets
  • Browsing history or visited URLs
  • Personally identifiable information (name, email, IP address)
  • Crash reports or usage analytics
  • Any data sent to an external server

PassChef operates no backend servers. There is no PassChef account, no sign-up, and no cloud service. The only "sync" that occurs is Google's own chrome.storage.sync, which syncs your encrypted secrets between your Chrome browsers. We have no access to this data — it is held by Google under their own Privacy Policy.

3. Chrome Permissions Explained

PassChef requests only the permissions necessary to function. Here is exactly what each permission is used for and why it is required:

storage

Used to persist your encrypted secrets and settings in chrome.storage.sync. This allows your encrypted configuration to sync across Chrome browsers where you are signed in. No plaintext data is ever written to storage.

clipboardWrite

Used to copy a generated password to your clipboard when you click "Copy" or trigger generation. The password exists in memory only during generation and is cleared from the clipboard automatically after 30 seconds via the alarms API.

activeTab

Used to read the content of the currently active Google Sheets tab — specifically the text of the selected cell, which contains the recipe formula (e.g., bank$3). Access is granted only for the current tab at the moment you trigger generation. The extension does not have persistent access to your browser tabs.

tabs

Used to detect whether the currently active tab is a Google Sheets page, so the extension can enable or disable the generation button appropriately. The extension reads only the tab URL to check for the docs.google.com/spreadsheets pattern. No tab content is read.

idle

Used to detect when your browser becomes idle, triggering the auto-lock feature. After 5 minutes of idle time, the extension locks and requires your master password to continue. No idle state data is transmitted anywhere.

alarms

Used to schedule the 30-second clipboard auto-clear. After copying a generated password, an alarm is set to overwrite the clipboard with an empty string after 30 seconds. Also used to trigger session timeout (auto-lock after 10 minutes of active session).

4. How Encrypted Data Is Stored

Your secret phrases are encrypted using industry-standard cryptography before any storage occurs:

  1. Your master password is processed through Argon2id (memory: 64MB, iterations: 3, parallelism: 4) to derive an encryption key. This process is intentionally slow and memory-intensive to resist brute-force attacks.
  2. Each secret is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, producing an authenticated ciphertext with a random nonce.
  3. Only the resulting ciphertext (encrypted noise) is written to chrome.storage.sync.

The encrypted data syncs to other Chrome browsers where you are signed in with the same Google account, via Google's own sync infrastructure. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. From PassChef's perspective, the data in Chrome Sync is opaque ciphertext — we cannot decrypt it, and neither can Google.

Important: Your master password is never stored anywhere — not locally, not in Chrome Sync, not on any server. If you forget your master password, there is no recovery mechanism. This is by design: if we could recover it, so could an attacker.

5. No Telemetry or Analytics

PassChef contains zero analytics, telemetry, error reporting, or usage tracking of any kind. There are no third-party SDKs, tracking pixels, or remote logging calls in the extension codebase. You can verify this by reviewing the open-source code.

The extension makes no outbound network requests except those initiated directly by you (for example, accessing Google Sheets in the active tab). There is no "phone home" behavior.

6. Data Retention and Your Control

You have complete control over all data associated with PassChef:

  • View data: All stored data is accessible via Chrome's developer tools (chrome.storage.sync) or within the extension settings.
  • Delete data: Use the "Reset Extension" option in the extension settings to clear all stored encrypted secrets.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the extension from Chrome removes all locally stored data. Note that Chrome Sync data managed by Google may persist independently; you can clear this from your Google account's Chrome sync settings.
  • Export: The extension does not provide export functionality. Your recipes live in your own Google Sheet, which you control entirely.

7. Your Google Sheets Data

PassChef reads the text of the currently selected cell in Google Sheets to extract the recipe formula. This reading happens:

  • Only when you are actively on a Google Sheets tab
  • Only at the moment you trigger password generation (click or keyboard shortcut)
  • Only for the selected cell content — not the entire spreadsheet

The cell text is processed in memory to generate the password and is immediately discarded. It is never stored, logged, or transmitted. The extension cannot see your actual passwords because they are not in the sheet — only the recipe formulas are.

Your Google Sheets file, its content, and its sharing settings are entirely under your control. PassChef does not require any special Google Sheets permissions and does not access the Google Sheets API.

8. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

Because PassChef does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal data on external servers, the formal data-processing obligations under GDPR and CCPA are largely not applicable. Nonetheless:

  • No personal data is sold or shared. There is no business model based on user data. PassChef is a free, open-source tool.
  • No data is transmitted to third parties. The only third-party infrastructure used is Google's chrome.storage.sync, which you access directly through your own Chrome/Google account.
  • Right to erasure: You can delete all extension data at any time by resetting or uninstalling the extension.
  • Right to access: All data stored by the extension is accessible to you directly through Chrome's developer tools.
  • CCPA: We do not sell personal information. California residents have all the same rights as all other users.

9. Children's Privacy

PassChef is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Given that PassChef collects no personal information from anyone, this is inherently satisfied.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted at passchef.io/privacy with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be noted in the extension's release notes. Continued use of the extension after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Given that PassChef collects no personal data, meaningful changes to this policy are unlikely.
