Pro Tips

Use Meaningful Hashes

Use the service name as hash (9mailR4nd0mHash, bank, netflix) — easy to remember and unique per site.

Categorize by Importance

Use different secret jars for different security levels: #1 for critical (bank), #3 for casual (social).

Rotate the Ingredient

Change the ingredient text, for example r4nd0m#1 to r4nd0m2#1, then update the actual account password. Avoid predictable rotation patterns for real accounts.

Strong Secrets Matter

Your secrets should be 8+ chars with mixed case, numbers, and symbols. They're the real keys to your kingdom.

Security

Cookbook Encryption

Argon2id derives the Cookbook key; AES-256-GCM protects stored profiles. These controls do not protect a compromised device, phishing or weak credentials.

Argon2id

Memory-hard key derivation raises the cost of guessing the master password. It protects the Cookbook; Recipe cooking is a separate operation.

Memory: 64MB Iterations: 3 Parallelism: 4

AES-256-GCM

Authenticated encryption protects stored profiles. A strong master password and correct key handling remain essential.

256-bit key Authenticated NIST approved

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PassChef.

A Menu holds Recipes rather than cooked Dishes, but can reveal account names and ingredient patterns. A visible verification Tag can help check guessed weak Spices offline. Keep sensitive Menus private and use strong Spices. Argon2id and AES-256-GCM protect the encrypted Cookbook stored through Chrome Sync; they do not make a public Menu universally safe.

Unfortunately, there's no recovery option — by design. This is zero-knowledge architecture. We never see or store your master password. You'll need to reset the extension and reconfigure your secrets. We strongly recommend writing down your secrets and storing them safely offline.

Traditional password managers store encrypted passwords in a vault. PassChef is different — we don't store passwords at all. We store recipes that combine with your secrets to generate passwords on demand. This means:

  • An encrypted Cookbook of Spices remains sensitive stored data
  • Works with your existing Google Sheets workflow
  • You control the Recipe for each Dish
  • Cooked Dishes are not saved as a list, but exist in memory and may reach the clipboard

Encrypted profiles use Chrome Sync according to your browser settings. Cross-device delivery, concurrent edits and recovery still need release acceptance; no universal device support is promised. Phone QR receiving/import is separate later work. Use a strong master password against offline guessing of stolen encrypted data.
