How PassChef works.

Keep Recipes in your Cookbook and private Spices encrypted on your device. Cook each Dish when you need it.

Section 01

The Idea

A Recipe combines an ingredient with one of your private Spices to cook a Dish. The Cookbook holds Recipes, not the finished passwords.

Your Spices are encrypted on the device and in Chrome Sync. You unlock the kitchen with your master password; Spices do not appear in the Cookbook.

Only Recipes are visible in the Cookbook.

Anyone with only the Recipe still lacks the private Spice needed to cook your Dish. Use long, unpredictable ingredients and strong Spices.

A Builder verification tag checks the Cookbook/profile match. It does not change the Dish; you can cook by hand without the tag.

Section 03

Modifiers

Place modifiers after the Spice jar number. They are deterministic, but stripping characters is not reversible. Combined Spice transformations run strip, reverse, then uppercase.

_flipSwitches topping # and base $; other styles stay the same.
!upperUppercases the Spice before cooking.
?reverseReverses the Spice before cooking.
~stripKeeps only A–Z, a–z and 0–9 in the Spice.
Recipe with modifiers:r4nd0m#1!?Spice jar 1 is reversed, then uppercased, before being placed first.
Section 04

Daily Use

Unlock PassChef, select a Recipe cell in your Google Cookbook, then open the extension. A valid Recipe cooks a Dish; missing data or a mismatch stays on Home with guidance.

01
Open your Cookbook

Keep Recipes in cells; private Spices stay encrypted separately.

02
Select a Recipe cell

Click a cell such as fb#1 or gh$2!, then open PassChef with its toolbar icon.

03
Unlock and cook

The icon opens the popup. Ctrl+Shift+L cooks the selected cell through the page flow; it is not the popup opener.

04
Copy and paste

Use Copy to put the Dish on the clipboard. PassChef attempts to clear it after 30 seconds; closing the popup alone does not clear the system clipboard.

The unlocked session locks after 5 idle minutes or 10 total minutes. Encrypted Spices can remain in Chrome Sync; this static demo reads no Cookbook or private Spice.
Rotating a Dish?Change the ingredient, for example fb#1 to fb2#1, then update the password on the account website yourself. Your Spice can stay the same.
