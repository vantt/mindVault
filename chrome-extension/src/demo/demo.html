Section 01

The Idea

Storing passwords in a spreadsheet means one breach exposes everything. mindVault flips this — you store recipes, not passwords. A recipe is a short code that tells the extension how to build your password, using a secret only you know.

Your spreadsheet can be public. The password is never stored anywhere — it's computed fresh each time from the recipe + your secret.

Section 02

Anatomy of a Recipe

A recipe looks like fb#1 — a hash you choose, a position symbol, and a secret index from 1 to 5. The hash is the only public part; the index points at one of your five phrases, never spelling it out.

Position symbols — hash = abc, secret = XYZ

Symbol Meaning Result
# Prefix — secret first XYZabc
$ Suffix — secret last abcXYZ
@ Middle — secret in centre aXYZbc
% Interleave 1-by-1 aXbYcZ
^ Interleave 2-by-2 abXYcZ
Section 03

Modifiers

Append optional modifier symbols after the position symbol to transform the output. Each modifier is deterministic and reversible — same recipe, same output, every time. Modifiers can be combined.

_ flip Reverses how the secret combines with the hash
! upper Uppercases the secret before combining
? reverse Reverses the secret before combining
~ strip Strips spaces and punctuation from the secret
Recipe with modifiers: r4nd0m#1!? ← hash r4nd0m, position #, secret 1, then modifiers !?
Section 04

Daily Use

Open a spreadsheet, click the cell with your recipe, then open mindVault. The popup auto-detects the cell and generates the password. Paste it, then close. Nothing was written to disk, nothing was sent over the network.

01
Open your Google Sheet

Your spreadsheet holds recipes — short codes like fb#1 or gh$2!.

02
Click a recipe cell

Anywhere on the spreadsheet — mindVault reads the active cell.

03
Open the extension

Click the icon or press Ctrl+Shift+L (Mac: ⌃⇧L). The popup auto-routes to the Generated screen.

04
Copy & paste

The password is on the clipboard. Close the popup and it disappears.

Nothing is stored, transmitted, or logged outside your device.
Rotating a password? Update only the recipe hash — e.g. change fb#1 to fb2#1. Same secrets, completely different password. No secret changes needed.
